Project Overview

The Liquor Store System is a centralized software solution designed to organize the daily operations of a liquor store. Currently, the business relies on non-centralized manual records and mobile phone data, causing difficulties in tracking inventory, customer debts, and cash flow



\*\*Core capabilities include:\*\*

\* \*\*Inventory Control:\*\* Updates available stock after each operation and organizes supplier data and purchase orders for restocking.

\* \*\*Credit Sales Management:\*\* Records customer credit purchases and payments to monitor accurate outstanding balances.

\* \*\*Cash Reconciliation:\*\* Distinguishes between owner and employee sales, tracking received payments and expenses to verify expected daily cash balances.

\* \*\*Automated Pricing:\*\* Calculates selling prices and gross profit according to business rules, automatically applying defined discounts.

\* \*\*Age Verification:\*\* Requires cashiers to confirm customer age checks before completing sales.
