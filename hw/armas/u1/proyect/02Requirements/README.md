\## Project Overview

The Liquor Store System is a Java-based console application designed to manage the daily operations of a liquor store, utilizing local JSON files for data persistence. The system centralizes information about products, inventory, suppliers, sales, and cash movements to support daily management decisions and maintain accurate records. 



\*\*Core capabilities include:\*\*

\* \*\*Inventory \& Supplier Management:\*\* Registers products, tracks stock entries and exits, sets minimum stock alerts, and manages purchase orders and supplier deliveries.

\* \*\*Sales \& Customer Credit:\*\* Records cash and credit sales, identifies the responsible seller (owner or employee), and tracks customer debt payments and outstanding balances.

\* \*\*Financial Tracking:\*\* Supports daily cash reconciliation by comparing expected closing cash with physically counted cash, and calculates gross profit and margins based on applied percentage discounts.

\* \*\*Reporting \& Compliance:\*\* Generates reports on best-selling products for selected periods and displays an automatic age-verification reminder before completing any sale.
